"Everyone has a story" is a phrase that's become a little too precious, Hallmark even. But Every Human Ever doubles down and convincingly provides every single humans life story. Now, it gives its best guess based on data available for the largest percentage of humans, but it's sobering and heartwarming. My first human died at three in northern China due to a chest infection to parents who themselves only probably lived to twenty-four a thousand years ago. Makes you feel incredibly tiny, in a good way.
